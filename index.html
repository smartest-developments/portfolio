03 / OPERATING PRINCIPLES

Useful automation has to earn trust.

  1. 01

    Start with the job

    Technology follows the actual decision, handoff or repetitive task that needs to move.

  2. 02

    Keep a human boundary

    Automation should be observable, reversible and clear about where a person stays in control.

  3. 03

    Design for the Monday after launch

    Operations, maintenance and real-world exceptions are part of the product—not an afterthought.
